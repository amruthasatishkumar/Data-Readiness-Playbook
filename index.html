03 / ACT

Your next actions.

This is a self-assessment of the data supporting your use case, not a certification, compliance opinion, or approval to deploy AI.

How do these six scores work?

Each layer counts Yes answers / 3. Yes earns one point. No, Not sure and Unanswered earn zero, but stay distinct in your results. Notes and owner details are optional and do not change points.

  • Yes: you believe the control is in place. This is self-reported, not independently verified.
  • No: a known gap to address.
  • Not sure: investigate before claiming Yes.
  • Unanswered: still to review, not the same as No.

No blended AI-ready percentage or deployment pass. A critical question not answered Yes remains unresolved regardless of scores elsewhere. Even 3/3 in every layer needs independent validation, not automatic approval.

Days 01-30

Scope and verify

Validate the evidence with your owners. Resolve critical unknowns. Narrow the pilot where necessary.

Days 31-60

Strengthen and test

Address the priority gaps. Test realistic questions, permissions, stale data, exceptions, and escalation.

Days 61-90

Review and decide

Reassess with frontline experts. Decide whether to pilot, improve, redesign, or stop. These are planning horizons, not deadlines to launch.
